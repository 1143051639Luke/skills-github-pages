date: 2026-10-27

A useful research report should be reproducible from its data and code.
For FINM 32800, I am working with Treasury yield curves and Fed Funds futures.
The pipeline pulls data, estimates the models, executes the notebooks, and
builds charts and reports with a task runner.

GitHub Pages gives those generated reports a stable home on the web. This
practice blog helped me learn how branches, Jekyll configuration, and dated
Markdown posts become a published website.
